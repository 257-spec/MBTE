🥩 Nutrition

Prioritize animal protein and fat. Dairy: low-fat quark, raw milk, butter, ghee, cream. Raw, grass-fed, A2.

120g liver 2x weekly — immensely nutritious. Sardines 4–5x weekly for optimal omega ratio.

Cook exclusively in ghee. Extra virgin olive oil cold over food. I use coconut oil as a mouth wash, or moisturizer. Ghee, coconut, olive — all you need.

Fruits: oranges, bananas, kiwi, blackberries, pomegranate — polyphenols and clean glucose. Starches: potatoes, white rice, soaked oats — clean glucose, fiber, phytonutrients. Veg: carrots, onions, garlic, bell peppers, ginger, beets. Usually boiled or cooked. I eat raw carrot salad 3–4x a week.

Raw honey as a sweetener or for taste.

Time carbs pre/post workout. Protein post workout for mTOR. Carbs and fats don't mix — worsens insulin sensitivity. Carbs minimal or zero close to bed — insulin sensitivity lowest then. Sauerkraut or raw dairy for probiotics.

Sardines and organs give what other foods can't. I fast 16 hours occasionally.
