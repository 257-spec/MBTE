🌿 Environment

Avoid plastic. Stainless steel, wood, glass only. Polyester out — bamboo or cotton.

Seed oils? Unstable, oxidized, refined. Avoid entirely. Whole, simple foods — that's it.

Bone broth. Beef stew with gelatinous cuts. Pork or chicken skin. Raw colostrum — liquid fresh, grass-fed, or freeze-dried. Rich in antibodies, minerals, growth factors. Avoid industrial grain-fed.

Sunscreen? No. Seek sun at peak UVB, controlled moderate sessions. Progressive overload. Low PUFA, high antioxidant, sufficient saturated fat.
