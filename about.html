About

From struggling teenager to biology optimiser

At 14, I lived an unhealthy lifestyle — poor sleep, junk food, no structure, and micronutrient deficiencies. I felt tired, bloated, and constantly procrastinated.

Around 15, I discovered the "looksmaxing" trend on TikTok, which emphasized thyroid health and IGF-1. Something about it fascinated me, so I started digging deeper.

Over the next 1.6 years, I taught myself nutrition, osteology, and endocrine and sleep physiology at a cellular level. I began applying what I learned — optimizing my sleep, diet, supplements, and exercise routine.

The results were significant: less facial bloating, better mental clarity, improved bone structure, and increased muscle mass.

Inspired by my own transformation, I started creating content to share my knowledge. Since then, I've helped others improve their energy, sleep, and even overcome insomnia — something I struggled with myself.

My goal now is to explain not just the how and what, but the why behind it all.
